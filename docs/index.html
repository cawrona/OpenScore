Sheet music that keeps up with you.

OpenScore is a free, open-source PDF score reader for Linux. Turn pages with a pedal, mark up with a pen, and pick up exactly where you left off.

Library

What you played last is always on top.

OpenScore reads the folders you already have. Nothing is imported or locked away. Recent scores sit in a strip at the top, and a folder rises as soon as anything inside it is opened.

Rename or move a file in your file manager and its history and annotations follow, because scores are matched by their content.

What is in v0.1

Built for the music stand.

Presto

Instant page turns

MuPDF renders on its own thread and prepares the pages around you ahead of time, so a turn is a texture swap. Single and two-page views.

Andante

Your folders, as they are

Titles, composers, page counts and covers come from the PDFs. Files stay where you keep them.

Con moto

Moves keep their history

Recents and annotations are tied to the score, so renaming or relocating a file loses nothing.

Espressivo

Pen, highlighter, eraser

Pressure-sensitive ink with undo and redo. It is stored separately and never written into your PDF.

Legato

Pedals and MIDI

Bluetooth page turners work as keyboards. Any key or MIDI message can be learned in two clicks.

Dal segno

Back where you stopped

Open a score and you land on the page you left. Touch turns pages and only the pen draws, so palms never leave marks.

Pedals

Any pedal. Any key.

Most Bluetooth page turners present themselves as a keyboard, so they work the moment they pair. MIDI pedals and controllers are supported when OpenScore is built with RtMidi.

Try the learner on the right. Press Learn, hit a key, then press it again to see the action fire. An input can only do one thing, so assigning it moves it.

Click here and press a bound key to see it fire.
